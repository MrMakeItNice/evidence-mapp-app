Field guide / Evidence literacy

How do we know?

  1. Read the original record. Open the linked California State Parks description.
  2. Separate observation from interpretation. A documented fish trap or pictograph is evidence; its meaning may require further context.
  3. Ask whose perspective is missing. Archaeological interpretation should include living Indigenous communities.
  4. Protect the site. This map deliberately avoids sensitive archaeological coordinates.

Evidence labels: documented site description ≠ verified interpretation of every symbol, date, or cultural practice.

Editorial note: These are historical and cultural heritage profiles, not dated news events. They do not contribute to Global Evidence Pulse totals. Interpretations of Indigenous sacred traditions should be grounded in tribal voices; access restrictions and site protection take priority.
