Research privacy & transparency

Privacy and data access

How the current Evidence Atlas reader and owner workspace handle access to content.

Public reader

Visitors may read published case summaries, newsroom headlines and background material without a research-workspace account. Public content can be fetched from Edge Functions and third-party sources. Ordinary web hosting, network and security systems may process connection metadata; this notice does not claim to eliminate logs or cookies.

Private research workspace

Authorized researchers sign in through Supabase Auth. The browser application is configured to persist and refresh its session and may retain authentication data in browser storage. Supabase PostgreSQL row-level security restricts access to private case files, notes, research inbox items and drafts. The public source-code repository is separate from the private research database. No security control should be assumed infallible.

External source links

Evidence Atlas links to external agencies, news publishers and document archives. Those sites may use their own tracking, account, cookie and privacy practices, outside this project's control. Review their notices before sharing information.

Corrections, requests and contact

For project documentation see the public source repository. No separate public contact email has been established. Please do not transmit sensitive information about witnesses, survivors or private individuals via public issue threads.

Research limitations

Not all sources, articles or case assertions have been independently verified. Private submissions remain subject to review, and nothing in a document alone establishes culpability. This is a description of the present app architecture, not legal advice or a comprehensive lawyer-reviewed privacy policy.
