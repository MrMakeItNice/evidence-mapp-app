What is real today—and what comes next?

Implemented in repository: interactive globe preview, Giza tutorial, editorial reader, global search interface, paper-trail and connection navigation.

Existing backend records: 143 cases, 285 sources and 244 relationships at the November 11 snapshot. Counts are not verification or quality scores.

Still to validate: live public deployment, mobile browser behavior, full end-to-end source linking, review workflows, and production-ready data-driven globe relationships.

Our principle: a collected headline is a lead, not a verified claim. A relationship is not proof of wrongdoing.

This is a presentation-oriented tour of existing repository pages and a small interactive learning exercise. It does not represent an independently verified production deployment.
