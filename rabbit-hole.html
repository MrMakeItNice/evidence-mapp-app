🐇 The Rabbit Hole

A space for unconventional hypotheses, conspiracy theories, curious questions, and serious scrutiny of powerful institutions. Bring your curiosity. Bring your sources. Be willing to change your mind.

Freedom to question. Responsibility to distinguish evidence.

Make It Nice supports open expression and good-faith disagreement. Its central objective is to organize documented facts, compare competing explanations, and hold power accountable. The historical record gives us good reason to ask hard questions about whether authorities always act in the public interest. That does not make every suspicion true.

Suspicion is not proof. Correlation is not causation. An official explanation can be challenged without assuming an alternative explanation is correct.

No person is beneath respect. No institution is above scrutiny. No theory is above testing.

How a theory becomes a documented case

1. Question → 2. Testable claim → 3. Primary sources → 4. Independent corroboration → 5. Counterevidence → 6. Reviewed finding.

Every claim should carry a clear label: Speculation, Unverified, Disputed, Supported, or Established. Only reviewed, sourced material belongs in the factual case archive. A label can change when stronger evidence appears.
