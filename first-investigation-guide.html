OPEN INVESTIGATION / MULTIPLE EXPLANATIONS

Compare the theories

Explore documented observations, research interpretations, and unconventional proposals without confusing them. Each explanation faces the same questions: what supports it, what challenges it, and what would test it?

Research labels describe the type and strength of the available support, not whether a person is permitted to ask a question. “Unverified” does not mean “disproven.”
