Cover story · Global health

Tuberculosis deaths are falling worldwide

WHO’s 2026 report finds that global deaths from tuberculosis continued to decline in 2025. The global incidence rate also fell 14% from 2015 to 2025, though progress varied widely by region.

Report released November 12, 2026 · WHO global estimates

Why it leads. Fewer people dying from TB is measurable progress. WHO estimates that about 10.6 million people still developed TB in 2025.
