What would help right now?

01 / Check in with yourself

How does this moment feel?

Pick a word. It can be more than one thing; this is simply a starting point.

You do not have to earn rest. Start by noticing what you need.

This check-in is not a mental-health assessment.

02 / Come back to the room

Ground through your senses.

Let your eyes stay open. Notice your surroundings at a pace that suits you. Skip any sense that is unavailable or uncomfortable.

STEP 1 OF 5
5 things you can see.

Look around and name five ordinary things. You do not need to write them down.

Adapted from NHS inform grounding guidance ↗.

03 / A quiet minute

One minute with less to do.

Let your breathing stay natural. Rest your hands somewhere comfortable. You can simply look at one object in the room. There are no breath holds or targets to meet.

01:00

Stop or skip whenever you want.

04 / Make the next step small

Care can be ordinary.

  • Have a drink of water or a regular meal.
  • Make room for sleep and a steadier daily routine.
  • Try gentle movement, if comfortable and accessible.
  • Take a break from upsetting news or research.
  • Reach out to someone you trust.

Adapted from NIMH self-care guidance ↗. Choose one useful step, rather than trying to do everything.
