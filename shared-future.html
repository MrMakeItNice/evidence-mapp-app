Research integrity

Truth needs a method.

Evidence before conclusions

Primary sources, dates, methods, and source quality are visible. Claims can be corrected as evidence changes.

Patterns aren't plots

Shared interests, institutional overlap, and similar outcomes do not by themselves demonstrate secret coordination or malicious intent.

Spiritual freedom

Respect religious, secular, Indigenous, and other worldviews. Separate lived experience, philosophical interpretation, and testable scientific findings.

Accountability for everyone

Apply the same standards to governments, corporations, nonprofits, researchers, and the claims made by Evidence Atlas itself.

Freedom without uniformity

Cooperation must not require a single religion, culture, political identity, or explanation of reality.

Leave things better

Alongside documenting harm, examine credible reforms, measurable improvements, and solutions communities can use.
