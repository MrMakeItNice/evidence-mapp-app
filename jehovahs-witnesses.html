Discussion launch status: The community's purpose and safeguards are published here. Live member accounts, posting, moderation, and private messaging are not yet enabled. Do not submit sensitive personal stories or identifying details until privacy and safety controls are operational.

Community principles

Respect belief and nonbelief. No harassment, doxxing, threats, coercive proselytizing, or pressure to reveal religious status. Critique claims and institutions without attacking ordinary members. Protect minors and survivors; never publish identifying allegations about private individuals without a lawful, carefully moderated process.
