Our commitment

We aim to use support for necessary research, hosting, development, accessibility and reasonable living and operating needs of those doing the work. Our intention is to direct resources beyond those needs back toward people and communities that need them most.

We will work toward publishing clear summaries of project income, expenses and community contributions. These statements describe our goals, not an audited financial report or a legally binding allocation formula.

Evidence and core public-interest information remain available without a contribution. Financial support does not buy influence over research findings or editorial decisions.

Transparency and donor privacy

When enabled, payments will be processed by Stripe or PayPal on their hosted services. Evidence Atlas will not ask you to enter card numbers or payment passwords into this page. Please review each provider's terms and privacy notice before contributing.

Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations. Tax treatment depends on the recipient's legal status and applicable law. Do not submit sensitive personal information in donation messages.
