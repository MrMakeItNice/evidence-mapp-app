Follow the evidence.

Explore an investigation through a spacious, source-linked evidence map. Select a document card to inspect its original record. Select a document to see other cases citing the same source. A shared citation is not evidence that the cases or people are otherwise connected.

Keyboard: Tab to a diagram card, then use arrow keys to pan the view. Home centers the focused card without changing zoom. Escape stops an active drag. Enter or Space opens card details; Escape from details returns to the card, bringing it into view if needed.

Search by title or exact case ID, such as #42. From the diagram or details, Ctrl+K (Command+K on Mac) focuses search.
