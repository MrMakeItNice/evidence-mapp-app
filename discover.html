Start here · The field guide

Become your own investigator

Three steps to begin. The best investigator knows what they don't know.

01 · Ask

Begin with a question

Find a historical event or current story. Write down what you think you know and what could change your mind.

02 · Check

Follow the documents

Compare dates, reporting, official findings, and opposing evidence. An allegation is not a proven fact.

03 · Connect

See the bigger picture

Examine historical parallels without assuming causation. Consider constructive, measurable solutions.
