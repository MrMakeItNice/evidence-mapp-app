## Lead A — Food and agriculture cybersecurity oversight

- **Publisher:** U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO)
- **Report:** GAO-27-108656, *Critical Infrastructure Protection: Further Action Needed to Enhance Cybersecurity of the Food and Agriculture Sector*
- **Official primary-source URL:** https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-27-108656
- **Published / publicly released:** November 19, 2026
- **GAO's reported finding:** USDA and HHS/FDA had taken steps to address food and agriculture cybersecurity but partially met or did not meet five of the sixteen relevant requirements examined, particularly on information sharing and emergency preparedness. Both agencies cited limited staffing and resources.
- **Agency responses:** HHS agreed with GAO's three recommendations; USDA disagreed with two addressed to it and requested that GAO withdraw or redirect them. GAO maintained that these recommendations remain warranted. **Record this disagreement explicitly**, rather than reporting unanimity or proof of misconduct.
- **Scope/methodology caveat:** GAO reviewed agency actions against specific requirements under the FY2021 NDAA and interviewed officials plus a **non-generalizable sample of six private-sector organizations**. This does not establish the cybersecurity condition of every business in the sector.
- **Review questions:** Inspect the full agency comment letters, check whether any recommendation has since closed, obtain corroborating CISA/USDA/FDA primary materials and distinguish threats from documented incidents.
- **Suggested topics for human review:** Critical infrastructure; food and agriculture; cybersecurity; government oversight. **No automatic claim or case correlation.**

## Lead B — Information on military tuition assistance

- **Publisher:** U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO)
- **Report:** GAO-27-107854, *Military Education Benefits: DOD Should Take Steps to Ensure Service Members Receive Information About Tuition Assistance*
- **Official primary-source URL:** https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-27-107854
- **Published / publicly released:** November 20, 2026
- **GAO's reported finding:** The Air Force and Navy did not require bases to inform newly assigned personnel about tuition assistance at orientation, notwithstanding GAO's reading of the relevant DOD policy. Some personnel GAO consulted were not aware of this benefit.
- **Agency response:** DOD agreed with GAO's recommendations and described steps taken to implement them. On the official report page, the two recommendations were still listed **open** at review time.
- **Scope/methodology caveat:** GAO reviewed service policies and participation figures through FY2024, conducted four site visits and ten discussion groups involving 112 active-duty personnel. The sample is **not a national prevalence estimate** for unaware service members.
- **Review questions:** Verify precise DOD policy language, track responses and follow-up implementation, and distinguish insufficient orientation information from whether an individual qualifies for benefits.
- **Suggested topics for human review:** Educational access; personnel benefits; oversight. **No automatic case correlation.**
