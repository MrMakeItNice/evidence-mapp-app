# Evidence Atlas — approved visual direction
Approved by project owner on 2026-11-11. Treat this as the persistent design brief for future website and investigation UI work.

## Overall
Make Evidence Atlas hella cool, futuristic, cinematic, immersive, and interactive while preserving a deliberately old, rustic investigative magazine aesthetic. The contrast is the identity: futuristic intelligence exploration meets archival newspaper journalism. Avoid replacing the magazine with a generic modern SaaS dashboard.

## Three signature experiences
1. **3D Investigation Globe:** rotating Earth, elegant glowing geographic markers, animated arcs, location-linked investigations, hover/click to inspect a case and enter its evidence web. Decorative arcs must be explicitly differentiated from verified geographical relationships. Provide accessible non-3D navigation and mobile performance fallback.
2. **Rustic Investigative Magazine:** 1990s newspaper / aged newsprint / archival paper / ink / strong serif headlines / editorial grids / tactile clipping imagery; dramatic, compelling, accessible storytelling. Keep this section distinctly vintage rather than neon sci-fi. Link every story to dated source material and distinguish editorial demos from published findings.
3. **Webbed Paper Trail:** immersive full-screen, zoomable, pannable intelligence-style evidence graph with rich, multi-layered connections among cases, people, organizations, documents, dates, events, financial flows and locations. Click nodes and edges for source references, provenance, evidence status, counterevidence and unresolved questions. Filters, legend, search, time slider and drill-down. Build from real records; never invent relationships.

## Seamless visitor journey
Globe → magazine investigation → webbed evidence map → original sources; users can enter from any view and navigate back. Include a beginner-friendly Giza mystery tutorial as an approachable example.

## Evidence integrity
Use distinct edge types and styling for verified/documented links, attributed allegations, disputed links, and hypotheses; never imply coordination or wrongdoing from shared citations alone. Pending collector headlines are unreviewed leads, not verified facts. Avoid visualizations that fabricate data. Show honest loading/error/empty states.

## Build priorities
Preserve the existing working pages and Supabase-backed foundations. First establish a reliable public demo and a visually compelling webbed evidence experience using source-grounded records. Then connect the web and globe through verified locations and relationships. Confirm live deployment and browser testing before claiming features are public.

## Visual keywords
Cinematic dark navy, deep teal, luminous mint and amber accents for futuristic views; warm sepia, off-white, charcoal ink, paper textures, serif editorial typography for the magazine. Rich but readable; responsive; accessible motion controls.

This is a design specification, not a claim that all three experiences are already fully implemented.

## Owner refinement — 2026-11-11
The preferred Evidence Web is a **clean, sprawling investigative mind map with real photographs and document previews embedded inside nodes**, not a floating-photo constellation or a stylized detective corkboard. Keep a spacious, restrained dark canvas, organic graph layout, fine unobtrusive links, excellent legibility, and smooth zoom/pan. Click a photo or document to inspect its original source, date, evidence status, and competing interpretations. Prior image concepts were rejected as generic, overdecorated and excessively neon/sepia. Avoid decorative strings, fake news clippings, faux technical overlays, clutter, excessive glow and invented metrics. Keep the vintage/rustic look mainly in the separate magazine experience. Preserve the 3D globe as a separate discovery mode. Do not present concept imagery or unverified relations as actual sourced records.
