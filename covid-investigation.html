EVIDENCE LIBRARY

Research collections

Origins and early response

Zoonotic and laboratory-related hypotheses, intelligence assessments, early reporting, and unresolved evidence.

Illness and mortality

Case counts, excess mortality, long COVID, methods, and differences between reported and estimated outcomes.

Vaccine trials and effectiveness

Trial protocols, efficacy and effectiveness, product-specific evidence, and benefit-risk changes over time.

Vaccine safety and adverse events

Confirmed risks, surveillance signals, clinical investigations, and the distinction between events reported after vaccination and events caused by it.

Policies and civil liberties

Mandates, school closures, economic and mental-health effects, legal rulings, and proportionality.

Contracts, profits and accountability

Public procurement, corporate revenue, lobbying, conflicts of interest, lawsuits, and documented findings.

Vaccine composition and biological claims

Ingredient disclosures, manufacturing controls, independent analyses, and evidence needed to evaluate biological-warfare allegations without treating them as established facts.
